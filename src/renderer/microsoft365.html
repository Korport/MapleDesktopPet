Microsoft 365 notifications

Let Maple alert you about new Outlook Inbox email and Teams chats. Work or school accounts in the Microsoft public cloud only.

Organization-approved setup required

Ask your administrator for a single-tenant Microsoft Entra app registration. Under Authentication, enable Allow public client flows for device-code sign-in. No client secret or redirect URI is needed.

Add delegated Microsoft Graph permissions: User.Read, offline_access, Mail.ReadBasic for email, and Chat.ReadBasic for Teams. Only enable the sources you are authorized to use. Your organization may require admin consent or block device-code sign-in; Maple cannot bypass those policies.

Enter the application's client ID and your directory's tenant ID below. These IDs are not passwords. Never enter a client secret here or in chat.

Maple checks about once a minute while running, starting after sign-in; existing messages do not trigger alerts. Teams alerts reflect the latest incoming message per chat, so bursts may be grouped or missed if your own reply replaces the preview before the next check. These are arrival alerts, not an unread counter.

Alerts contain no sender names or message text. Microsoft Graph may return a Teams preview, but Maple discards its text and never sends it to GitHub Copilot or the activity log. Tokens and message IDs stay in memory and are cleared on disconnect or restart. Only the app IDs and source choices are saved locally; sign in again after restart. Disconnect stops Maple's monitoring, but does not revoke consent in Microsoft.
